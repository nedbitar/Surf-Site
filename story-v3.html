Story

2004 — Present
Chapter
One

Learning to surf at 18 on the shores of Oahu

A late start, but Mike Wallace made up for it with a vengeance. Drawn into the art of shaping boards for family and friends, he was influenced by legends: Marc Andreini, George Greenough, Michel Junod.

◆
Chapter
Two

The first board: a 5'10" fish inspired by Christenson

Following an interview on computer shaping with Matt Ambrose in Pacifica, the first shape emerged. Like that very first fish, Wallace took to shaping like water – designing boards eventually took over his life.

◆
Chapter
Three

Pushing boundaries with sustainable materials

Flax-basalt. Agave. Carbon-innegra. Epoxy bio-resin. Polyola. Grow Blanks. Continuously pushing the boundaries of what's possible in board design and environmental responsibility.

Chapter
Four

Shaping guns for Mavericks and beyond

Now collaborating with 3-time World Surf Champion Grant "Twiggy" Baker, and Mavericks chargers Wilem Banks and Steve Rice. Edge board guns refined for the world's heaviest waves.

The Philosophy

"I feel as if I'm part of a collective of like-minded shapers seeking to innovate and improve our lot and move up the food chain to more enlightened practices."

Chapter
Five

The future: 3D printed closed-loop manufacturing

Collaborating with SwellCycle on 3D printed boards using recycled plastic hospital trays. A proud participant in Sustainable Surf's ECOBOARD Project. The evolution continues.
