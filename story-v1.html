The Journey

From learning to surf on Oahu to shaping boards for world champions

← Scroll to explore →

01
🌊

The Beginning

At 18, Mike Wallace learned to surf on Oahu. A late start, but he made up for it with a vengeance – eventually coaching the Half Moon Bay high school surf team.

02
🔧

First Shapes

Influenced by Marc Andreini, George Greenough, and Michel Junod, Mike shaped his first board while writing for SurfPulse. A 5'10" fish inspired by Chris Christenson.

03
🏆

Recognition

Placed twice in the Boardroom Show's "Best in Show" – tying for first with a finless model and top three for an innovative flax-basalt twin fish.

04
🌿

Innovation

Experimentation with flax-basalt, agave, carbon-innegra, and epoxy bio-resin. Pushing boundaries with Polyola, Grow Blanks, and sustainable materials.

05
🦈

Big Wave

Now collaborating with 3x World Champ Grant "Twiggy" Baker and Mavericks chargers Wilem Banks and Steve Rice on edge board guns.

06
🔮

The Future

Collaborating with SwellCycle on 3D printed boards using recycled plastic hospital trays. Part of Sustainable Surf's ECOBOARD Project.
